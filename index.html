How this model works

Read range starts from the radiated power (reader power plus antenna gain, minus cable loss), then allows 3 dB for a circular antenna reading a linear tag. Tag size and how the item is carried add or remove signal. As a reference, a 6 dBi antenna at 30 dBm reaches about 20 ft to a standard tag in open hand.

The footprint on the floor comes from that range and the distance between the antenna and the tag height. It's stretched or squeezed by the antenna's beamwidth, so a wide-beam antenna spreads its zone and a narrow one concentrates it. Bi-static AN440 wiring adds a small sensitivity gain. "Works from" is the lowest power with full coverage, clear direction, no shelf reads, and radiated power inside the region limit.

Antenna figures are typical EU-band values from Zebra's antenna family data. FX7500 maximum power is shown as about 31.5 dBm; confirm it on the current datasheet. Real read zones are irregular because of glass, metal frames, floors, and people, so treat these numbers as a planning starting point and confirm the layout with a walk test on site.
